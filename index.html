How it works

Set up in under a minute

No accounts, no API keys, no port forwarding. If your iPhone and miners share a Wi-Fi network, you're ready.

Scan your network

BlockSeeker finds compatible miners on your Wi-Fi automatically, or add any miner by IP address.

It groups them for you

Miners are sorted by type and algorithm, each with the right units: TH/s, GH/s or kSol/s.

Watch, get alerts, act

Live stats, alerts when something goes wrong, and one-tap fixes for one miner or a whole group.

Support

Questions? I answer every email.

Email TechBitsMix@gmail.com for help, bug reports or feature requests.

My miners aren't showing up.

Make sure your iPhone or iPad is on the same Wi-Fi network as your miners and allow local-network access when iOS asks. Then tap Scan, or add a miner by its IP address.

Which devices are supported?

Bitaxe, NerdAxe, NerdQAxe and NMAxe (AxeOS); Avalon Nano 3 and 3S; Goldshell Mini-DOGE; and Antminer units on stock, Vnish, Braiins OS or LuxOS firmware.

Do alerts work when the app is closed?

Yes, on a best-effort basis. iOS decides how often background checks run, so alerts can be delayed. Keep Notifications and Background App Refresh on for BlockSeeker.

Can BlockSeeker break my miner?

Every control uses the miner's own official settings API, the same one its web interface uses. Auto-recovery is off by default and reboot attempts are capped.

Does BlockSeeker collect my data?

No. It works entirely on your local network with no account and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Is there a subscription?

No. BlockSeeker is a one-time purchase.
